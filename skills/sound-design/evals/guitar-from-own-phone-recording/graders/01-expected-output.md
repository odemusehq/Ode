The result matches this description: Ode proposes isolating the guitar from the musician's own recording, says honestly which artefacts to expect (drum machine bleed, smeared or soft transients, a watery tone) and how to clean them with ordinary tools (EQ, trimming, fades or gating). It notes that because the recording is the musician's own it is cleared for release, and keeps a note of the source for the credits. It checks the guitar's pitch against D minor and transposes or detunes as needed, and deals with the tempo difference (92 to 86 BPM, drifting) through a sensible warp mode choice or by playing it from Simpler. It picks a Simpler mode with a reason (Slicing to play phrases from keys, or Classic for melodic notes) and gives starting settings such as mono playback, start points and short fades. For the dusty version it gives concrete devices and starting values (for example Redux, Saturator or Roar, Auto Filter band-limiting, Vinyl Distortion or a separate noise texture), kept as a separate, bypassable option. It works in small steps, explains each briefly and asks the musician to listen and approve.
