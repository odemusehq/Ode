# Hip-hop drums

Only what is specific to hip-hop. The loop (read, decide, change in place, verify, let them hear it, report in one line), grid notation, velocities, the note map and the general checks are in the drums skill.

## Leave room for the voice

Rap is rhythmically dense, so the drums give the pocket and leave the words room. Keep the mid-range clear, keep patterns steady enough for the rapper to lock to, and save the busiest moments for the gaps between lines — read the vocal's notes or the recorded vocal's clips to find them.

## Boom bap

- Around 84–96 BPM, sixteenths swung about 55–62%, a hard snare on 2 and 4 that sits slightly late, and a syncopated kick around it.
- Dusty, sampled-sounding drums; ghost kicks and ghost snares fill the gaps.

```
        1 e + a 2 e + a 3 e + a 4 e + a
Hat     x . x . x . x . x . x . x . x .
Snare   . . . . X . . g . . . . X . . .
Kick    X . . . . . . x . . X . . . . .
```

Kick on 1, the "a" of 2 and the "and" of 3. In bar 4 add a kick on the "a" of 4, or drop the hats for the last beat, to turn the phrase around.

## Trap

- Around 130–160 BPM, felt in half-time: the snare or clap lands on beat 3 of each bar, so the groove feels like 65–80 BPM.
- Hi-hats in eighths or sixteenths, broken up with rolls — bursts of thirty-second notes or sixteenth-note triplets — and occasional open hats. Rolls often rise or fall in velocity or pitch.
- The kick (usually the 808 itself) is sparse and syncopated; space is part of the sound.

```
        1 e + a 2 e + a 3 e + a 4 e + a
Hat     x . x . x . x . x . x . x . x .
Clap    . . . . . . . . X . . . . . . .
808     X . . . . . . x . . X . . . . .
```

On beat 4, replace the hats with a roll: six sixteenth-note triplets, or eight thirty-second notes.

## Drill and lo-fi

- **Drill**: around 140 BPM with a half-time feel, sliding 808s, triplet hats and percussion, and snares placed off the plain backbeat in syncopated patterns. Keep the hats busy and the kick sparse.
- **Lo-fi**: slower (about 70–90 BPM), heavy swing, soft kicks, rim or brushed snares, and worn, filtered sounds.

## The 808

In trap and drill the 808 is both kick and bass; its notes are written with the bass skill (tuning, note length, slides). If a separate kick plays too, tune it to the 808 and place the two so they hit together or alternate and stay distinct.

## Stops, switches, hooks and verses

- **Stops**: the drums drop out for a beat or a bar under a punchline or the last word of a line, then come back on the downbeat — remove those notes from the clip in place.
- **Beat switch**: a new pattern, tempo feel or kit for the last verse or the outro gives contrast without a bridge. It is new material for that section, so it goes in that section's own clip.
- **Hooks** (usually 8 bars) can add elements: open hats, extra percussion, layered claps. **Verses** (usually 16 bars) stay sparser so the words read clearly; vary the pattern every four bars.

## Velocity

- Boom bap: steady, hard snares, softer ghost notes, swung hats with uneven accents.
- Trap: rolls that rise or fall in velocity, steady claps, 808 velocity mostly constant.

## Checks for hip-hop

Run these with the drums skill's checks.

| Check | Correct when |
|---|---|
| Room for the voice | fills, rolls and extra hits land clear of the start of each key word in the vocal; the busiest moments fall in the gaps between lines |
| Snare placement | 2 and 4 for boom bap; beat 3 for half-time trap and drill feels |
| Variation | the pattern changes slightly at least every four bars |
| 808 and kick | a separate kick and the 808 start together or alternate; each one's tail stays clear of the other |
| Rolls | rolls sit on weak beats or the end of a bar, and their velocities rise or fall |

## Result lines

- "Hat rolls on beat 4 of bars 2 and 4, rising in velocity · clap still on 3, rolls clear of the rapper's lines."
- "Stop under the punchline in bar 8: drums out for beat 4, back on the downbeat · lands on 1, the rest as it was."
- "Boom bap swing to 58% on hats and ghost snares, snare a touch late · kick still with the bass, loop ends clean."
