# Prosody

Ask lyricist with the real words, exact melody and intended delivery. Consider meaning, stress, rhyme and musical setting together. A speech-like stress pattern or balanced form is an option, never a universal requirement.

When fitting a vocal guide, supply tempo/meter, lyric revision, phrase origin and exact note starts/durations. Ask for a word/syllable-to-note plan that considers lexical and intended semantic stress, metric placement, pitch peaks, relative durations/intensity, vowels, consonant transitions and breaths. Counting syllables alone cannot establish whether a phrase can be sung naturally. Singing may stretch a vowel, compress a weak syllable, use melisma or deliberately stress a function word; interpret the artist's meaning rather than applying one fixed rule. State pronunciation or placement uncertainty and do not describe an unheard singer as verified.
