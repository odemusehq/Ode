The result matches this description: Ode declines to imitate another singer's voice, explains
briefly that it only uses the artist's own voice, and offers that instead. Before saving or using
the recording on "Voice Ref", it confirms the musician's clear consent and explains in plain words
what will be kept (a short, clean recording of their own singing, in their own Ode folder, with a
record that they agreed), that it is used only to sing their demo vocals, and that they can withdraw
consent at any time. It says the demo vocal is a guide for writing and recording and that the
released vocal is normally their own performance, and that the musician keeps the rights to their
song and the demo, with any model used required to allow commercial use and to leave them the rights
(without naming any provider or model). Before making the demo it checks, or says it will check,
that the lyric is set to the melody, the prosody works and the melody sits in their range, starts
with the chorus, and plans to put the demo on a new, clearly named audio track without touching
existing vocals or clips. It tells the musician what to listen for (range, prosody, phrasing and
breath, lyric clarity).
