(calm, an empty late-night drive home after a shift) instead of assuming that F minor means sad, and

labelled as a departure from the musician's usual style or from the mood they described. It does not
invent facts about the musician (their job, relationships or background) beyond what they said, may
note that the slow tempo and space suit fewer, longer words, asks at most one question, and does not
present sample lines as checked unless a check actually ran.
