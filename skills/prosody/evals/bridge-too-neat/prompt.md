Here's my bridge. The song is about finally leaving the town I grew up in, and the bridge is meant to be the moment I actually go. It feels too neat and the last chorus doesn't hit after it. Also, the highest note in the whole song is on "it" at the end of line 5. Chorus is four lines, ABAB, mostly 8 syllables a line.

I packed the car before the light
And didn't stop to say goodnight
The petrol station sign went by
I didn't even wave goodbye
I'm leaving and I'm sure of it
I'm finally the one who quit
